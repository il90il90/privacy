ScoreUp — Privacy Policy

App package: com.scoreup.app (phone and Wear OS)
Developer: Israel Cohen
Effective date: 28 October 2026

In short
  • ScoreUp is a sports scoreboard for phones with a Wear OS watch remote and TV display. We have no server and no accounts and receive none of your data.
  • On the watch, ScoreUp can read heart rate (with your permission) while the app is open. It is shown on your own devices and saved with your scores on your devices only; it is never sent to us or used for ads.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no tracking.

Who we are

ScoreUp is developed and published by Israel Cohen, an individual developer ("we", "us"). We do not operate any server for this app and do not receive any data from it.

Heart rate (health data)

The Wear OS app asks for the Body sensors permission at first launch. If you allow it, ScoreUp reads your heart rate from the watch sensor only while ScoreUp is open on the watch (it stops when the app is paused) and can be switched off in the watch controls. Heart rate is used only to display it during a match and to keep the average and peak with a saved score. It is never sent to us, never sold or shared with third parties, and never used for advertising. If you deny the permission, everything else works without it.

How your devices talk to each other

  • Phone ↔ watch sync uses Google's Wear OS Data Layer (Google Play services). Score events and live heart rate are passed between your paired devices. Depending on how your devices are connected, Google Play services may relay this traffic, encrypted, through Google's infrastructure; it is not accessible to us.
  • TV: the scoreboard can be shown on an external display or via screen casting. If you configure a native Google Cast receiver, the phone sends the match state (team names and colours, scores, sets, clock) to your Chromecast device using Google Cast (Google Play services).
  • Watch installer: the phone can install or update the watch app over your local Wi-Fi using the watch's Wireless debugging, after you pair with the 6-digit code. This stays on your local network.

Internet use

For copies installed outside Google Play, the phone app checks the project's public GitHub releases (api.github.com) for a newer version and downloads it from GitHub only if you choose to update. This request contains no personal information; GitHub sees your IP address as with any web request.

Permissions

  • Watch: Body sensors — heart rate (optional, see above). Vibrate, wake lock — haptics and keeping the scoreboard visible.
  • Phone: Internet, network/Wi-Fi state, multicast — update check, Cast, and finding the watch on your network for installation.
  • Phone: Vibrate — haptics. Install packages — only for an update you chose to install (sideloaded copies).

ScoreUp does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos.

Children

ScoreUp is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We collect no data from anyone.

Your choices and rights

We hold no personal data about you. You can revoke the Body sensors permission in the watch's settings at any time, turn heart rate off in the watch controls, or uninstall the app (or clear its data) to remove everything.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be reflected here before, or at the same time as, the app version that introduces them.
