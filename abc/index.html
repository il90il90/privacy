ABC — Hebrew & English Letters (אבג ABC) — Privacy Policy

App package: com.meylon.abc
Developer: Israel Cohen
Effective date: 28 October 2026

In short
  • A letter-learning app for young children (ages 3–6). It collects no personal information.
  • The app has no internet permission and works fully offline: nothing can be sent off the device.
  • No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no tracking.

Who we are

This app is developed and published by Israel Cohen, an individual developer ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. The app does not request the Android Internet permission and contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or payment SDK. It has no sign-in, no chat and no way for a child to enter or send personal information.

Information stored only on the device

WhatWhyLeaves the device?
Chosen alphabet (Hebrew or English) and sound on/offRemember the child's settingsNo
Star count and which letters were openedShow progressNo

This is kept in the app's private storage. It is deleted when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. If the device owner uses Android's device backup, it may be included in that owner's own Google backup; we never receive it.

Sound and speech

Letters and words are played from recordings built into the app or spoken by the device's own text-to-speech engine. The app does not use the microphone. If no voice is installed for a language, the app may offer a shortcut to the device's text-to-speech settings.

Permissions

The app requests no special permissions. It only asks Android which text-to-speech engine is available.

Children's privacy

The app is designed for children and is intended to comply with the Google Play Families Policy, COPPA and the GDPR (including Article 8). It collects no personal information from children or anyone else, shows no ads and has no purchases or external links.

Your choices and rights

We hold no personal data about you or your child. Uninstall the app, or clear its data in Android Settings, to delete everything it stored.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be reflected here before, or at the same time as, the app version that introduces them.

Contact

Questions or requests about this policy or about your data: il90il90@gmail.com (Israel Cohen).
