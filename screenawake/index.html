Screen Awake — Privacy Policy

App package: com.screenawake.app
Developer: Israel Cohen
Effective date: 28 October 2026

In short
  • Screen Awake keeps your display on. It has no internet permission, so it cannot send any data off your device.
  • No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking.
  • Your settings and, if you enable it, the list of apps you picked are stored only on your device.

Who we are

Screen Awake is developed and published by Israel Cohen, an individual developer ("we", "us").

Information we collect

None. Screen Awake does not collect, upload, sell or share personal data. The app does not request the Android Internet permission, contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and has no account or server.

Information processed only on your device

Settings are stored in the app's private storage. If you have Android's device backup turned on, your settings may be included in your own Google backup; we never receive it. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps — to hold an invisible keep-screen-on window and the optional floating button.
  • Usage access (optional) — only for Selected apps mode, to know which app is on screen. Not used otherwise.
  • Notifications, foreground service, wake lock — to show the ongoing status notification with a Turn off action and keep the screen on.
  • Run at startup — optional resume after the device restarts or the app updates.
  • Quick Settings tiles and shortcuts — to toggle the feature from the notification shade or a shortcut.

Screen Awake does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. Tapping the developer link in the app opens it in your browser or Telegram, outside the app.

Children

Screen Awake is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Your choices and rights

We hold no personal data about you. You can revoke any permission in Android Settings at any time, and uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be reflected here before, or at the same time as, the app version that introduces them.
