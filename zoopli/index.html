Zoopli — My First Animals — Privacy Policy

App package: com.zoopli.app
Developer: Israel Cohen
Effective date: 28 October 2026

In short
  • Zoopli is an educational app for young children. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
  • No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting, no tracking, no chat or social features.
  • Settings and game progress are stored only on the device. The only internet use is downloading the app's own animal pictures and sounds.

Who we are

Zoopli is developed and published by Israel Cohen, an individual developer ("we", "us"). Contact details are at the bottom of this page.

Information we collect

None. Zoopli does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal or sensitive information. The app has no sign-in, and contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, crash-reporting SDK, payment library or social features. There is no way inside the app for a child to enter or send personal information.

Information stored only on the device

This data lives in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app deletes it, and a parent can clear progress at any time from the Parents area. If the device owner has turned on Android's own backup, the settings and progress may be included in that owner's Google device backup; that backup belongs to the device owner and is handled under Google's terms — we never receive it. Downloaded content is excluded from backup.

Internet use

The app's only network requests download Zoopli's own animal content (a list of files, then the pictures and sounds) from a public content repository:

  • cdn.jsdelivr.net (jsDelivr CDN), and as a fallback
  • raw.githubusercontent.com (GitHub)

These are plain file downloads. No account, identifier, device information or usage data is sent with them. As with any download, the hosting provider receives the device's IP address and a generic app User-Agent as part of the connection and may keep it in its own server logs under its own privacy policy; we do not receive or use those logs. After content is downloaded the app works offline, and it checks for new content occasionally.

Optional “learn more” button (assistant apps)

If one of a small set of AI assistant apps (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Copilot, Perplexity or Grok) is already installed on the device, an animal page shows a button that opens that app with a pre-written request for a short, child-friendly story about the animal. To know whether to show the button, Zoopli checks only whether those specific apps are installed (it cannot see any other apps). Zoopli itself does not connect to any AI service and sends nothing: pressing the button simply hands the text (the animal's name and facts already shown in Zoopli) to the other app, which is the device owner's own app, signed in to the owner's own account and governed by that app's privacy policy. The button is not drawn at all when none of those apps is installed. The button is intended for a parent reading with the child.

Permissions

  • Internet / network state — to download the animal content and to know whether the device is online.
  • Vibrate — short haptic feedback during games (can be turned off).

Zoopli requests no runtime (“dangerous”) permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or storage access. Animal names may be spoken using the device's built-in text-to-speech engine, which runs on the device.

Children's privacy

Zoopli is designed for children and is intended to comply with the Google Play Families Policy, the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU GDPR (including Article 8). Because the app collects no personal information, there is no child data to sell, share, profile or use for advertising. There are no ads and no purchases. The Parents area (settings, progress and content management) is behind a simple arithmetic question so that a young child cannot change settings unsupervised.

Third-party content

Animal photographs, illustrations and sounds are third-party works used under free licences (CC0, public domain, CC BY and CC BY-SA), each credited inside the app under Parents → Credits.

Your choices and rights

Since we hold no personal data about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can delete everything stored by the app by clearing progress in the Parents area or by uninstalling the app.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be reflected here before, or at the same time as, the app version that introduces them.
