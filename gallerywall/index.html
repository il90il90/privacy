Gallery Wall — Privacy Policy

App package: com.il90.gallerywall
Developer: Israel Cohen
Effective date: 28 October 2026

In short
  • Gallery Wall turns a TV or tablet (the Display) into a photo frame controlled from your phone (the Remote).
  • We have no server and no accounts and receive none of your data. No ads, no analytics, no crash-reporting service, no tracking.
  • Your photos, videos and music travel only between your own devices over your local Wi-Fi. Optional features you turn on (art, music, news, weather, shared-album import, updates) contact the public services named below.

Who we are

Gallery Wall is developed and published by Israel Cohen, an individual developer ("we", "us"). We do not operate any server for this app and do not receive any data from it.

You can delete media from the Remote (individual items or “Clear all”). Uninstalling the app on a device deletes everything it stored there.

Local network

The Remote and Display find each other with Android network service discovery and talk directly over your Wi-Fi using HTTP on your local network. This traffic stays on your network and is not encrypted; use the app on a network you trust. The optional screen PIN stays on the Display.

Optional internet features

These run only when you use them. Each request goes directly from your device to the service named; we are not involved and receive nothing. Like any internet request, the service sees your IP address and may log it under its own privacy policy.

Gallery Wall does not use your device's location; weather is based only on the place you type.

Face detection

To orient and crop photos automatically, the Display can detect where faces are in a picture. This runs on the device using Google ML Kit (via Google Play services) or Android's built-in face detector. Images are not uploaded for this. Google Play services may download the detection model and may handle limited diagnostic information under Google's own terms.

Permissions

  • Internet, network/Wi-Fi state, multicast — local-network discovery and transfer, and the optional internet features above.
  • Foreground service (media playback / data sync), notifications, wake lock — keep the frame and music running, and finish uploads to the Display in the background, with a visible notification.
  • Run at startup — optional: relaunch the Display after the TV box powers on.
  • Install packages — only for installing an update you chose to download (sideloaded copies).

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, microphone or camera. On the phone, media you send is picked by you through Android's own picker.

Children

Gallery Wall is a general-audience app and is not directed at children. We collect no data from anyone.

Your choices and rights

We hold no personal data about you. You control what is sent to your Display and can delete it at any time; every optional internet feature can be left off.

Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change. Material changes will be reflected here before, or at the same time as, the app version that introduces them.
